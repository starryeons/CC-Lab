[ The Dream Fish ]

Scientific Name: Solitarius Piscis

The Dream Fish

Discovered by/at:

Jacques Piccard, Mariana Trench,Jan. 23, 1960

Family: Somniosidae

The dream fish displays traits of somniosidaes, sleeper sharks. This is characterized by slow movements, low levels of activity, and generally non-aggressive nature.

Habitat: Aquatic Aphotic Zones

The Dream Fish tends to be found in deep ocean environemnts where light cannot reach and other forms of life is rarely found. Occassionally, it can be found in shallow undisturbed water, but primarily lives in saltwater.

Appearance:

This most prominent feature of the Dream Fish is its large size and uncanny, human-like face and wide toothless grin. It lacks gills, but sports colorful scales across its flesh. Its skeleton can be observed through the skin of the creature, giving it a haunting appearance.

Key Characteristics:

  • Can grow up to 5 feet long
  • Aggression levels are unknown
  • Consumes undersea plankton.
  • Bioluminescent skin and antennae

What we know so far about [ The Dream Fish ]? While officially classified as a somniosidae, the Dream Fish is presumed to have existed long before this evolutionary branch. No fossils of any Dream Fish have been found, so the lifespan and length of existence is still unknown. Theories across the web suggest that the Dream Fish is of supernatural or extraterrestrial origin, though these are unsubstantiated. Despite its lack of contact with humans, the Dream Fish strangely shows no fear of human presence. It is not known if this is due to its lack of intelligence or if it is a sign of its curiosity, though its unsettling appearance creates the sense that its lack of fear is of a more malignant origin. Only about 13 Dream Fish have been found across the world and have been left undisturbed. Due to the distance between each of these sightings and its lack of much movement, it is assumed that the Dream Fish do not reproduce. However, it seems that the area around which a Dream Fish lives teems with bioluminescent flora and glowing undersea rocks growing in strange, unnatural patterns. Due to these habitats being found in the aphotic zone, it is assumed that the Dream Fish is responsible for this phenomenon. Perhaps due to its mysterious nature, many theories online attribute the fish to mysticism, believing that scales fallen off the fish or even its bones can be used to make wishes. This is unconfirmed. It's earliest appearance was documented in a waterlogged diary presumed to be jetsam from the RMS Titanic shipwreck, which describes a strange glowing mermaid following the ship the day it sank.
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature Being unaccustomed to humans, the Dream Fish tends to follow the user around and shows visible excitement when someone gets close. This friendly demeanor is only offset by the menacing glow of its antennae, warning humans not to stray to close to this unknown creature. Due to its poor eyesight, be wary of touching it, as even a light tap may be dangerous.
